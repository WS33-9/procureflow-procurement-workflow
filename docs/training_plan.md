# Proposed training plan

## Goal

Help each internal role complete its work confidently while keeping formal approvals, transactions, contracts, supplier communication, and official records in their existing processes.

## Audience and outcomes

| Audience | By the end of training |
|---|---|
| Procurement officers | Find assigned work; maintain dependencies, next actions, dates, approvals, references, and closure; interpret attention reasons. |
| Procurement managers | Assign work; review workload and unassigned requests; use attention context for follow-up and escalation. |
| Administrators or support owners | Understand controlled values, data ownership, issue triage, and the limits of the prototype configuration approach. |

External requestors, approvers, and suppliers do not need ProcureFlow accounts in the first phase.

## Format

1. **Twenty-minute workflow overview** — business problem, five statuses, waiting-state treatment, and system boundaries.
2. **Thirty-minute role exercise** — complete the intake-first walkthrough in a training workspace.
3. **Fifteen-minute management review** — reconcile the request record to Dashboard workload and attention results.
4. **Questions and readiness check** — capture unclear terminology, missing guidance, and support needs.

## Training materials

- concise quick-start guide;
- [intake-first walkthrough](officer_demo_walkthrough.md);
- field and controlled-value reference;
- role/action summary;
- examples of approval and external-reference boundaries;
- support contact and issue-reporting steps.

## Readiness check

Participants should be able to create and locate a request, explain its owner and status, identify the next action, update current work, record an external confirmation/reference, close work correctly, and explain why a separate reporting update is unnecessary.

Training delivery, attendance, and client acceptance have not occurred. The final plan should be adjusted after UAT feedback and role decisions.
